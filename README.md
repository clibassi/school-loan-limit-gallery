# School Loan Limit Gallery

Examples of US colleges and programs that set their own lower federal student loan limit, or that
lend their own money to students, after the federal loan limits that took effect on July 1, 2026.
Each example shows the school's page with the key sentence highlighted, a plain explanation, and a
link to the source.

- These are examples, not a count. The gallery makes no claim about how common any of this is.
- Screenshots were captured on November 24 and 25, 2026 and show what each page said then. Schools
  may have changed their pages since.
- Explanations and classifications were written with an AI model from each school's page. They were
  not individually checked by a person. Where a classification is the project's reading and not the
  school's statement, the example says so.
- Screenshots are of public web pages and documents; each links to its source. Copyright in those
  pages stays with the schools and publishers.

The site is one static page (`index.html`) plus the screenshots in `shots/`.
